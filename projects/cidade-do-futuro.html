Project Overview

Ambivalência is a large-scale animated installation created for projection at the Alfândega Congress Center (Porto), developed as part of a collaboration between ESMAD and OCUBO. The project explores the emotional tension between a utopian dream and a dystopian reality, told through the eyes of a young protagonist.

Recognized as the Best Project at Immersivus X 2024 by public vote, the animation stood out for its immersive quality, strong visual contrast, and narrative impact.

Concept & Narrative

The story unfolds in two mirrored realities: a flourishing, peaceful utopia and a bleak, broken dystopia. These represent the duality between our dreams and fears of the future. Porto's architectural icons — like the Luís I Bridge and Clérigos Tower — are subtly reimagined in both worlds to create familiarity for local audiences and universal emotional depth.

Character in Utopia – vibrant and serene

Character in Dystopia – muted and tense

"The fundamental concept behind 'Ambivalência' is to create an immersive experience that plunges participants into a universe contrasting the utopian beauty of a dream and the dystopian cruelty of lived reality."

Visual Language

The visual style is defined by extreme contrast. The utopian world is painted with organic forms, glowing lights, and nature-driven palettes (greens, blues, browns). The dystopian world uses angular geometry, static compositions, and industrial tones (greys, purples, sickly yellows).

Utopia: Organic, glowing, nature-infused

Dystopia: Rigid, mechanical, polluted

Creative Approach

Animation Techniques

Puppet tool for characters and organic elements, hierarchical rigging for robots and structures.

Post-Production Effects

Glow, turbulence, wave warp and deform tools were used to enhance atmosphere and movement.

Immersive Transitions

Eye-blink inspired fade-ins helped guide viewers between dream and reality seamlessly.

Spatial Simulation

Used OCUBO's 3D Alfândega template for full-scale testing and camera walkthroughs.

Sound Design

Custom soundscapes blending ambient serenity with tension and chaos, timed to visuals.

Tools

After Effects, Photoshop, Procreate, MIRO, OCUBO Simulator
