**Purpose**: Advanced tracking script for your live Netlify website

- ✅ Scroll depth analysis

**Purpose**: Firebase data reader for your backoffice

- ✅ Reads analytics directly from Firebase

- ✅ Real-time updates
- ✅ Caching for performance
- ✅ Device/browser/location statistics

## 🚀 **Quick Setup (5 Minutes)**

### Step 1: Add Script to Your Live Website
Add this to your `other-projects.html` and other portfolio pages:

```html
<!-- Add before closing </body> tag -->
<script type="module" src="firebase-portfolio-analytics.js"></script>
```

### Step 2: Update Your Backoffice Analytics
Your backoffice will automatically use Firebase data instead of local files.

### Step 3: Deploy and Test
1. Upload `firebase-portfolio-analytics.js` to your Netlify site
2. Update your HTML files to include the script
3. Visit your live website
4. Open your backoffice - you'll see real visitor data!
